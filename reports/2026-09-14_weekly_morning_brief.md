11. **Samsung Electronics：与Mistral合作，将本地AI用于半导体流程。** 重要性：AI进入芯片研发和制造流程，效果取决于部署、数据治理及工程验证。[mistral](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-and-mistral-ai-announce-strategic-partnership-for-intelligence-driven-semiconductor-infrastructure)
